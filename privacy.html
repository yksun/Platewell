Platewell Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Platewell (“the app”) is a food, water and supplement log made by Yukun Sun, an individual developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your log stays on your iPhone, there is no account, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it.

What the app stores

Meals and their photos, notes you type, water, weight, supplements, saved foods, your profile (name if you give one, sex, age, height, goal weight, activity level, optional background, eating plan) and settings are stored only on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see this data. The app also creates a random ID on your iPhone so that an optional account can be added in a future version; it is not linked to you, your device or your Apple ID, and it is not sent anywhere.

If you use the Platewell widgets or the Apple Watch app, a short summary of today (water, calories, supplements) is shared with them on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch only.

No tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not collect device identifiers or your location.

AI features (only when you choose them)

Some features estimate nutrition from a photo or a short note, read labels, or write advice. You choose how each one works:

  • Apple Intelligence (including Image Playground pictures) runs on your iPhone. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Your own Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key. When you tap an AI button and agree, the app sends only what that request needs, directly from your iPhone to that provider. Depending on the feature this can be a meal or label photo, the note you typed, the foods and nutrition in your log, your eating plan, goal weight and activity level, the profile values you typed yourself, morning weights you entered in the app, and your targets. The developer never receives it. The provider’s own privacy policy applies: Anthropic, OpenAI. Your API key is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Copy and paste. The app copies a prompt (with the same kind of content) so you can paste it into any AI app yourself. Nothing is sent by Platewell; the AI app you use applies its own privacy policy.

Values read from Apple Health are left out of all of these requests and prompts (see below). The app asks for your permission before it first sends anything to an AI service, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → About.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. With your permission the app can:

  • Write water, weight and the nutrients of your meals and supplements to Apple Health, when you turn on sync.
  • Read your weight (to fill in days you did not log it) and today’s active energy (to raise your water goal on active days), when you turn on sync.
  • Read your sex, date of birth, height and latest weight, only if you tap “Fill in from Apple Health” while setting up your profile.

Values read from Apple Health are used only inside the app. They are never included in AI requests or prompts you copy, never sent to the developer or any other third party, and never used for advertising or marketing. If you change a value the app filled in from Apple Health, it counts as your own entry from then on. Targets the app calculates on your iPhone, such as your daily calorie and water goals, can take Apple Health data into account; those target numbers may be part of an AI request you start.

Barcode lookups

If you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. No other information is sent.

Backup

Backup creates a file only when you tap Back Up, saved where you choose (for example iCloud Drive). The file contains your log, including any weights imported from Apple Health, so keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a file you pick. The app has no automatic cloud sync.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. Data written to Apple Health can be managed in the Health app, where you can also turn off Platewell’s access at any time.

Not medical advice

Platewell is for general wellness and food logging. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: sunyukun9@gmail.com.

好好吃 Platewell 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

好好吃（Platewell）是个人开发者孙玉坤（Yukun Sun）制作的饮食、喝水和补剂记录 App。简单说：你的记录只保存在你的 iPhone 上，不需要账户，除非你主动使用，否则不会发送任何数据。

保存的内容

餐食及其照片、你输入的文字备注、喝水、体重、补剂、常用食物、个人资料（选填的名字、性别、年龄、身高、目标体重、活动量、选填的族裔背景、饮食方案）和设置都只保存在你的设备上。开发者没有服务器，看不到这些数据。App 还会在 iPhone 上生成一个随机编号，供以后的版本添加可选账户使用；它与你本人、设备或 Apple ID 无关，也不会发送到任何地方。

如果你使用好好吃的小组件或 Apple Watch App，今天的简要数据（喝水、热量、补剂）只会共享给本机小组件和与你配对的 Apple Watch。

不追踪

没有广告、没有统计分析、没有追踪，也没有第三方 SDK。不收集设备标识符或位置。

AI 功能（仅在你选择时）

部分功能会根据照片或一句话估算营养、读取标签或给出建议。每项功能的使用方式由你选择：

  • Apple 智能（包括 Image Playground 生成图片）在 iPhone 本机运行，数据不离开设备。
  • 你自己的 Claude（Anthropic）或 OpenAI API 密钥：当你点 AI 按钮并同意后，App 只把这次请求需要的内容从你的 iPhone 直接发送给该服务商。视功能不同，可能包括餐食或标签照片、你输入的文字、记录中的食物和营养数据、饮食方案、目标体重和活动量、你自己填写的个人资料、你在 App 里记录的晨起体重，以及你的各项目标。开发者不会收到。适用服务商自己的隐私政策（Anthropic、OpenAI）。API 密钥保存在设备的 iOS 钥匙串中。
  • 复制粘贴：App 复制提示词（内容同上），由你自己粘贴到任何 AI App。好好吃不发送任何内容，你使用的 AI App 适用其自己的隐私政策。

从 Apple 健康读取的数值不会出现在以上任何请求或复制的提示词中（见下文）。第一次向 AI 服务发送数据前，App 会征求你的同意；你可以随时在 设置 → 关于 中撤回。

Apple 健康

Apple 健康是可选的。经你允许，App 可以：

  • 开启同步后，把喝水、体重以及餐食和补剂的营养素写入 Apple 健康。
  • 开启同步后，读取体重（补上你没有记录的日子）和今天的活动能量（在活动量大的日子提高喝水目标）。
  • 只有在设置个人资料时点“从 Apple 健康填写”，才会读取你的性别、出生日期、身高和最近的体重。

从 Apple 健康读取的数值只在 App 内使用，绝不会放进 AI 请求或你复制的提示词，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方，也不会用于广告或营销。如果你修改了从 Apple 健康填入的数值，之后它就算作你自己填写的资料。App 在 iPhone 上计算的目标（例如每日热量和喝水目标）可能参考 Apple 健康数据，这些目标数字可能出现在你发起的 AI 请求中。

条码查询

扫描条码时，只把条码数字发送给 Open Food Facts（openfoodfacts.org）查询商品。

备份

只有在你点“备份”时才生成文件，保存到你选择的位置（例如 iCloud 云盘）。备份文件包含你的记录，也包括从 Apple 健康导入的体重，请妥善保管。没有自动云同步。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

删除数据

在 App 内删除记录，或删除 App 即可清除设备上的全部数据。写入 Apple 健康的数据可在“健康”App 中管理，你也可以随时在那里关闭好好吃的访问权限。

非医疗建议

好好吃用于一般健康和饮食记录，不是医疗器械，不提供医疗建议。
