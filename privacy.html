Platewell Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Platewell (“the app”) is a food, water and supplement log made by Yukun Sun, an individual developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your log stays on your iPhone, there is no account, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it.

What the app stores

Meals and their photos, water, weight, supplements, saved foods, your profile (sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, eating plan) and settings are stored only on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

No tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not collect device identifiers or your location.

AI features (only when you choose them)

Some features estimate nutrition from a photo or write advice. You choose how each one works:

  • Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Your own Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key. When you tap an AI button and agree, the app sends the photo or text needed for that request (for example a meal photo, a label photo, or a summary of the foods in your log and your profile and targets) directly from your iPhone to that provider. The developer never receives it. The provider’s own privacy policy applies: Anthropic, OpenAI. Your API key is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Copy and paste. The app copies a prompt so you can paste it into any AI app yourself. Nothing is sent by Platewell.

The app asks for your permission before it first sends anything to an AI service, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → About.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health sync, the app can write water, weight and nutrients to Apple Health, and read your weight and active energy. If you tap “Fill in from Apple Health” when setting up your profile, the app reads your sex, date of birth, height and latest weight. Data read from Apple Health is used only inside the app (to fill in your profile, to fill weight on days you did not log it and to adjust your water goal). It is never sent to AI services or any other third party, and never used for advertising.

Barcode lookups

If you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. No other information is sent.

Backup

Backup creates a file only when you tap Back Up, saved where you choose (for example iCloud Drive). Restoring reads a file you pick. The app has no automatic cloud sync.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. Data written to Apple Health can be managed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Platewell is for general wellness and food logging. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: sunyukun9@gmail.com.

好好吃 Platewell 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

好好吃（Platewell）是个人开发者孙玉坤（Yukun Sun）制作的饮食、喝水和补剂记录 App。简单说：你的记录只保存在你的 iPhone 上，不需要账户，除非你主动使用，否则不会发送任何数据。

保存的内容

餐食及其照片、喝水、体重、补剂、常用食物、个人资料（性别、年龄、身高、体重、目标体重、活动量、饮食方案）和设置都只保存在你的设备上。开发者没有服务器，看不到这些数据。

不追踪

没有广告、没有统计分析、没有追踪，也没有第三方 SDK。不收集设备标识符或位置。

AI 功能（仅在你选择时）

  • Apple 智能在 iPhone 本机运行，数据不离开设备。
  • 你自己的 Claude（Anthropic）或 OpenAI API 密钥：当你点 AI 按钮并同意后，App 会把这次请求所需的照片或文字（例如餐食照片、标签照片，或记录中的食物、你的资料和目标的摘要）从你的 iPhone 直接发送给该服务商，开发者不会收到。适用服务商自己的隐私政策（Anthropic、OpenAI）。API 密钥保存在设备的 iOS 钥匙串中。
  • 复制粘贴：App 复制提示词，由你自己粘贴到任何 AI App，好好吃不发送任何内容。

第一次向 AI 服务发送数据前，App 会征求你的同意；你可以随时在 设置 → 关于 中撤回。

Apple 健康

开启健康同步后，App 可以把喝水、体重和营养素写入 Apple 健康，并读取体重和活动能量。设置个人资料时如果点“从 Apple 健康填写”，App 会读取你的性别、出生日期、身高和最近的体重。从 Apple 健康读取的数据只在 App 内使用（填写个人资料、补上没有记录的体重、调整喝水目标），绝不会发送给 AI 服务或任何第三方，也不会用于广告。

条码查询

扫描条码时，只把条码数字发送给 Open Food Facts（openfoodfacts.org）查询商品。

备份

只有在你点“备份”时才生成文件，保存到你选择的位置（例如 iCloud 云盘）。没有自动云同步。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

删除数据

在 App 内删除记录，或删除 App 即可清除设备上的全部数据。写入 Apple 健康的数据可在“健康”App 中管理。

非医疗建议

好好吃用于一般健康和饮食记录，不是医疗器械，不提供医疗建议。

变更与联系

政策如有变更会在此页更新日期。联系：sunyukun9@gmail.com
