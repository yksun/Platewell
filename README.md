# Platewell · 好好吃

**A simple, private food log for the eating plan that suits you.**
**简单、私密的健康饮食记录 App。**

Platewell is an iPhone app by Yukun Sun for logging meals, water, supplements and weight, and seeing how your day fits your eating plan.

## Features

- **Photo meal log** – snap a meal and get calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sodium and up to 30 vitamins and minerals per food. Works with Apple Intelligence on device, your own AI API key, or copy-and-paste into any AI app.
- **13 evidence-based eating plans** – balanced, Mediterranean, DASH, MIND, flexitarian, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, Portfolio, low-carb, keto, high-protein and GLP-1 support, plus optional intermittent fasting.
- **Daily plan score** – see which food groups you ate and what is still missing.
- **Reviews and insights** – daily advice and weekly or monthly reviews from your own log, with checks for protein, fiber, sodium, saturated fat, added sugar and supplement upper limits.
- **Water and supplements** – a water goal from body weight, activity and climate with smart reminders; photograph a supplement label once and log doses with one tap.
- **Apple Health** – two-way sync for water, weight and nutrients.
- **Private by design** – no account, no ads, no trackers. Your log stays on your iPhone; manual backup to iCloud Drive.
- **9 languages** – English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português.

## 功能

- **拍照记餐**：拍一张照片，得到每种食物的热量、三大营养素、纤维、钠和多达 30 种维生素矿物质。支持 Apple 智能、自己的 AI 密钥，或复制粘贴到任何 AI App。
- **13 种饮食方案**：均衡、地中海、DASH、MIND、弹性素食、蛋奶素、纯素、鱼素、Portfolio、低碳水、生酮、高蛋白、GLP-1 辅助，可搭配间歇性断食。
- **每日方案评分、回顾与建议**、喝水目标与提醒、补剂一键记录、Apple 健康双向同步。
- **隐私优先**：无需账户、无广告、无追踪，数据只保存在 iPhone 上。
